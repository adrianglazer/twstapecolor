This run supplements the earlier Hong Kong verification with **populated US Level 2 books**. TWS PID 17419 was freshly running without agent hooks at baseline, on OpenJDK 25.0.2. XLAB was initially selected; TCRT was added at the user's request. No orders, subscriptions, link preferences or account settings were changed. Market captures and raw logs remain in ignored project `work/logs/us-v319/` and `logs/`.

## Changes

`start --debug` now enables a bounded asynchronous quote/trade CSV trace, in addition to retaining the command report. Each trade records its frozen quote, bid/ask sizes, local times, record/event identities and calculated direction. Changed top-of-book snapshots are logged independently of visible tape rows. Four 4 MiB segments and a 4,096-entry nonblocking queue bound each session; status exposes losses and writer errors. Stop disables and drains capture. Normal commands do not enable continuous logging. Old sessions are not automatically deleted.

The CSV covers watched tape records and incoming callbacks, not every market instrument, depth update, historical/backfilled row or unchanged quote notification. Identities are JVM-local and are not ticker symbols. The independent Python verifier detects gaps and checks nearest preceding per-record quote prices, sizes and sequence, then calculates direction with a decimal oracle. Data loss, missing initial quotes, no trades or mismatches prevent a complete pass. This checks local TWS sequencing, not an independently sourced exchange NBBO or true aggressor flag.

Runtime helper names advance to V0319 so TWS cannot accidentally reuse older production classes. The graphics-only painter, quote classifier, size curve, native-reset sorter safeguards, MIT license and debug opt-in behavior are preserved.
